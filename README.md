# MitFrauSchmidt

**AI phone assistant for trades and small businesses in Germany.**
Website: [mit-frau-schmidt.de](https://mit-frau-schmidt.de)

Frau Schmidt answers every call, spots emergencies and books appointments into your calendar. Every evening the owner gets a daily report via WhatsApp. Built for plumbers, electricians, roofers, car repair shops, hair salons and other small businesses. Call and customer data is stored on servers in Germany, and the AI introduces itself as an AI.

## What it does

- Answers every incoming call, also after hours
- Books appointments straight into the calendar
- Recognises emergencies based on the signals of each trade, for example a burst pipe or a gas smell, and alerts the on-call team
- Sends a daily report via WhatsApp
- Optional modules: email draft replies (sent only after approval), website chat, knowledge vault with your own documents, shop integration for Shopify and PlentyONE, social media posts
